The club’s first expedition north, in planning: three days in Abisko National Park, with birch forest, the Abisko valley and the Njullá ridge. Members hear first when the dates are set.

02Route24 km · +961 m

The route.

  1. Day 1 · 10.3 km

    Up the Abisko valley

    Abisko Turiststation → Abiskojaure

    We arrive on the morning train, walk through the Abisko canyon and follow Kungsleden along the river to the Abiskojaure mountain hut.

  2. Day 2 · 6.4 km

    Across the open fell

    Abiskojaure → Njullá shoulder

    We leave the marked trail and cross the open fell above the tree line. We stay in a hut or in tents, depending on the conditions.

  3. Day 3 · 7.1 km

    Njullá and the journey home

    Njullá → Abisko Turiststation

    With a certified guide we climb the Njullá ridge (1,169 m), then walk down to Abisko for the evening train to Stockholm.

05Packing list10 items

What to bring.

  • Shell jacket and trousers
  • Insulated jacket
  • Two base layers, wool
  • Hat, buff, two pairs of gloves
  • Broken-in boots, gaiters
  • Headlamp, spare batteries
  • Sleeping bag to −5 °C
  • Thermos, 1 l water
  • Sit mat, spoon, mug
  • Map and compass, the club brings the rest
